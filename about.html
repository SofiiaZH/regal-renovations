About Regal Renovations

Good building
starts with care.

Your home should work for you.

At Regal Renovations, we believe your home should work for you — comfortable, functional and designed around the way you want to live.

Led by Licensed Building Practitioner Dale Gommersall, with over 20 years of experience in residential and commercial construction, Regal Renovations has the experience and expertise to deliver everything from kitchens, bathrooms and outdoor living to extensions, structural alterations and complete home renovations.

We understand that renovating can involve a lot of moving parts. That’s why we offer complete project management, coordinating the trades, suppliers and different stages of your renovation, while providing one point of contact from start to finish. Our aim is to take the stress out of the process and make your renovation as straightforward as possible.

Every project starts with the client. We take the time to understand what you want from your home, how you use the space and what’s most important to you. From there, we offer practical advice and work with you to create a solution that suits your home, your lifestyle and your budget.

Ultimately, our goal is simple: quality workmanship, a well-managed project, and a home that feels better to live in.
